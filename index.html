Ultrafast terahertz electrodynamics of quantum materials (Unique)

We are an interdisciplinary research group dedicated to quantum physics and advanced spectroscopy. By bridging quantum materials, ultrafast optics, and chip-scale electronics, we develop state-of-the-art THz platforms to probe the hidden dynamics of low-dimensional systems. Our mission is to uncover the fundamental physics governing low-energy electrodynamics, many-body correlations, and nonequilibrium transport in condensed matter.

Our lab is based at the Aerospace Information Research Institute, Chinese Academy of Sciences (GBA branch), Guangzhou. We welcome inquiries from candidates interested in opportunities as undergraduate students, master's students, Ph.D. students, postdoctoral researchers, engineers, and assistant professors. Candidates interested in ultrafast spectroscopy, low-dimensional materials, and low-temperature quantum transport are welcome to contact us at thz_zheng@163.com .
