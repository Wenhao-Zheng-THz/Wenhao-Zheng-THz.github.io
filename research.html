Research

We investigate frontier problems in condensed-matter and quantum physics by developing advanced ultrafast and terahertz spectroscopy platforms across optical, electrical, and chip-integrated length scales.
Research Vision

Exploring frontier quantum physics through advanced spectroscopy

Our research seeks to understand how charge carriers, collective excitations, and correlated quantum states evolve across ultrafast time scales and low-energy scales. We focus on nonequilibrium quantum dynamics, many-body interactions, low-energy electrodynamics, and emergent electronic states in low-dimensional and quantum materials.

To address these questions, we develop, integrate, and continuously optimize advanced spectroscopic platforms, including transient absorption spectroscopy, optical-pump–terahertz-probe spectroscopy, broadband terahertz time-domain spectroscopy, and chip-integrated terahertz spectroscopy.

Frontier Physics Questions

Our platforms are designed around the physical questions rather than a single material system or measurement technique.

01

Nonequilibrium Quantum Dynamics

How do photoexcited carriers, excitons, and collective modes form, relax, and redistribute energy after ultrafast excitation?

  • Carrier generation, trapping, and recombination
  • Energy transfer across electronic and lattice subsystems
  • Transient photoconductivity and mobility
02

Many-Body Interactions

How do electron–phonon, electron–electron, and electron–hole interactions govern microscopic transport and optical response?

  • Carrier–phonon coupling
  • Excitonic and Coulomb correlations
  • Collective excitations and coherent modes
03

Correlated and Emergent Quantum States

How do superconductivity, electronic correlations, disorder, interfaces, and dimensionality reshape low-energy electrodynamics?

  • Complex conductivity and collective response
  • Temperature- and field-dependent quantum states
  • Mesoscopic and spatially confined electrodynamics

Spectroscopy Platforms

We build and optimize complementary platforms spanning visible, near-infrared, terahertz, electrical, cryogenic, and chip-scale measurements.

Ultrafast Optical Spectroscopy

Transient Absorption Spectroscopy

Broadband transient absorption spectroscopy tracks the evolution of excited states, excitons, polarons, and interfacial charge transfer with femtosecond temporal resolution. It provides access to population dynamics and electronic transitions over visible and near-infrared energy scales.

Ultrafast Terahertz Spectroscopy

Optical-Pump–Terahertz-Probe Spectroscopy

Optical-pump–terahertz-probe spectroscopy directly measures transient complex photoconductivity after photoexcitation. By connecting ultrafast dynamics with low-energy charge transport, it reveals carrier mobility, scattering, localization, and many-body coupling on femtosecond-to-nanosecond time scales.

Transient Photoconductivity Carrier Mobility Scattering and Localization Many-Body Coupling
Optical-pump–terahertz-probe spectroscopy platform
Chip-Integrated Spectroscopy

On-Chip Integrated Terahertz Spectroscopy

Chip-integrated terahertz spectroscopy confines and guides broadband terahertz pulses through microscopic transmission structures. It enables low-energy spectroscopy of micron-scale samples and devices that are inaccessible to conventional free-space terahertz measurements.
