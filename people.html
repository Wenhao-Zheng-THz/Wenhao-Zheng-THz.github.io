People

We develop ultrafast terahertz spectroscopy and chip-integrated platforms to investigate low-energy electrodynamics, many-body interactions, and nonequilibrium transport in low-dimensional quantum materials.
Wenhao Zheng (郑文浩)
Principal Investigator · Professor · Ph.D. Supervisor

Research Interests

  • Ultrafast optical-pump terahertz-probe spectroscopy: nonequilibrium charge-carrier dynamics
  • Low-temperature transport and chip-integrated terahertz spectroscopy: correlated electronic states in quantum materials, including superconducting systems
